LocalInk 1.0.1
==============

A local, offline macOS menu-bar app for drawing and directing attention on top
of your desktop.

Install
-------

1. Drag LocalInk.app into your Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications or Spotlight. A small LocalInk window appears with
   a Start Drawing button. Click it to begin, or use the pencil icon in the menu
   bar. LocalInk can also be closed to just its menu-bar icon.

First launch and macOS Gatekeeper
---------------------------------

LocalInk 1.0.1 is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized with Apple. The
first time you open it, macOS may say it cannot verify the developer.

Download LocalInk only from the official GitHub releases page. If macOS blocks
the first launch:

  - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" next to
    LocalInk.
  - On macOS versions that support it, right-click LocalInk.app, choose Open,
    then click Open again.
  - Or, in Terminal:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LocalInk.app

Only override the warning if you trust the download source. You only need to do
this once.

Using LocalInk
--------------

- Click Start Drawing in the LocalInk window, or click the pencil icon in the
  menu bar and choose Toggle Drawing.
- Toggle drawing with Control-Option-D at any time. Choose Welcome & Controls
  from the menu to reopen the LocalInk window.
- While drawing: P pen, A arrow, L line, R rectangle, O ellipse, H highlighter,
  T text, X redact, N counter, E eraser, V select. C cycles colors, [ and ]
  change width, and holding Command temporarily clicks through.
- Halo and spotlight follow the pointer while the app underneath stays clickable.
- Zoom and image export are optional and ask for Screen Recording permission.
  Drawing, halo, and spotlight never need it.

Privacy
-------

LocalInk has no account, telemetry, or network connection. Drawings stay in
memory for the session and are lost when the app quits unless you export them.

Uninstall
---------

Quit LocalInk from its menu, then move LocalInk.app to the Trash. Optional
preferences can be removed with:
    defaults delete dev.local.localink
