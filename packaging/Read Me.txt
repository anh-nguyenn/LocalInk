LocalInk 1.0.0
==============

A local, offline macOS menu-bar app for drawing and directing attention on top
of your desktop.

Install
-------

1. Drag LocalInk.app into your Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications or Spotlight. LocalInk appears as a pencil icon in
   the menu bar (it does not open a normal window).

First launch and macOS Gatekeeper
---------------------------------

LocalInk is a free, open-source app that is code-signed locally but not
notarized with an Apple Developer account. The first time you open it, macOS may
say it cannot verify the developer.

To open it anyway:

  - Right-click (or Control-click) LocalInk.app, choose Open, then click Open
    again.
  - On recent macOS versions, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click
    "Open Anyway" next to LocalInk.
  - Or, in Terminal:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LocalInk.app

You only need to do this once.

Using LocalInk
--------------

- Click the pencil icon in the menu bar to choose tools, colors, effects, export,
  and settings.
- Toggle drawing with Control-Option-D, or use the menu.
- While drawing: P pen, A arrow, L line, R rectangle, O ellipse, H highlighter,
  T text, X redact, N counter, E eraser, V select. C cycles colors, [ and ]
  change width, and holding Command temporarily clicks through.
- Halo and spotlight follow the pointer while the app underneath stays clickable.
- Zoom and image export are optional and ask for Screen Recording permission.
  Drawing, halo, and spotlight never need it.

Privacy
-------

LocalInk has no account, telemetry, or network connection. Drawings stay in
memory for the session and are lost when the app quits unless you export them.

Uninstall
---------

Quit LocalInk from its menu, then move LocalInk.app to the Trash. Optional
preferences can be removed with:
    defaults delete dev.local.localink
